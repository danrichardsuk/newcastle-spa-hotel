# Research / source notes

Research expanded on **2 September 2026** into an exhaustive candidate-universe audit rather than a conventional “best spa hotels” search.

## Coverage

The audit contains **78 plausible hotel-spa candidates** across:

- North East England / Northumberland / County Durham / Tees Valley
- Yorkshire including Harrogate, York, North Yorkshire and the western edge of the search radius
- Cumbria and the Lake District
- Scottish Borders
- Dumfries & Galloway
- Edinburgh / East Lothian / West Lothian
- the southern edge of central Scotland where normal drive estimates remain around three hours

Discovery was widened across:

- official regional and hotel tourism sources
- Tripadvisor / Google / Booking-style independent hotel evidence where indexed
- spa specialists such as Good Spa Guide, SpaBreaks and SpaSeekers
- deal-led discovery including Wowcher-style properties
- country-house hotels, golf resorts, leisure hotels and health-club models that may not market themselves primarily as destination spas

## Database structure

`spa-data.js` contains:

- **24 ranked high-quality recommendations** scoring 84+
- **Matfen Hall / Aurelius Spa** as a forthcoming, unranked record

`spa-candidates.js` contains the other **53 audited candidates**:

- 15 scored value / high-secondary options
- additional secondary hotel spas
- leisure-led properties
- niche formats that are not directly comparable with a communal destination spa
- Gleneagles as a traffic-sensitive three-hour boundary case

The public UI opens on the 24 recommendations but can display all 78.

## Eligibility approach

The recommended list favours properties with:

- on-site overnight accommodation
- therapist-led treatments
- a meaningful wet / thermal proposition beyond a basic gym and swimming pool
- credible independent guest evidence
- a spa experience integrated enough to justify a dedicated spa break

Lower tiers are retained rather than deleted. This means a hotel can be below destination-spa quality yet remain useful because of location, price or deal availability.

Third-party or club-style spa models are flagged rather than automatically rejected. For example, Radisson Blu Durham houses NuSpa inside the hotel, while Bannatyne Durham is closer to a hotel + health-club model.

## Latest analytical score weighting

The exhaustive audit uses:

- 30% spa depth and facilities
- 25% independent guest evidence
- 15% hotel quality
- 10% spa integration and serenity
- 10% value for the total experience
- 5% Newcastle accessibility
- 5% evidence / operational confidence

Score bands:

- 90+ exceptional destination spa
- 84–89 high-quality recommended shortlist
- 78–83 good/value alternative
- below 78 primarily leisure/value-led
- forthcoming properties remain unscored

## Operational / interpretation notes

- **Matfen Hall:** Aurelius Spa is scheduled to open 15 September 2026; the new spa remains unranked until meaningful post-opening evidence exists.
- **Rockliffe Hall:** recent refurbishment has affected parts of the spa / treatment proposition; current inclusions should be checked before paying.
- **Feversham Arms:** 2026 renovation messaging means Verbena Spa availability needs checking against the travel date.
- **Middleton Lodge:** deliberately low spa capacity is excellent for serenity but makes advance booking important.
- **Lodore Falls:** spa access can be sold in timed sessions rather than unrestricted all-day use.
- **Cairndale:** the spa is adults-only (18+).
- **SCHLOSS Roxburghe:** family-friendly; quiet adults-only thermal time should not be assumed.
- **Gleneagles:** route estimates straddle the three-hour cutoff, so it is retained only as a boundary case.
- Prices are indicative research estimates, not live quotes.
- Static coordinates and drive times are planning aids; use the site’s Google Maps Directions links for live routing.
- Some lower-tier properties do not have a fully standardised current review score because indexed review-platform coverage was incomplete. The site says so rather than inventing precision.
