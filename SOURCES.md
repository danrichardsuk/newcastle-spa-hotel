## Independent review approach

Tripadvisor is used as the common independent hotel-rating source where a current listing can be reliably verified. Review counts are retained alongside the /5 rating because evidence strength differs substantially between properties.

## Quality cutoff

The public ranking is intentionally **not exhaustive**. A property needs an on-site hotel, therapist-led treatments and a meaningful wet/thermal spa proposition, then must remain competitive on the combined analytical framework.

Additional researched properties that physically qualify but were not promoted into the main 20 include:
- Slaley Hall — substantial leisure/spa facilities but a weaker current independent hotel rating (~3.8/5)
- Feversham Arms & Verbena Spa — credible spa, but weaker rating and 2026 renovation uncertainty
- The Daffodil Hotel & Spa — genuine spa but less thermal depth than the strongest Lake District options
- The Balmoral / Irene Forte Spa — exceptional hotel, but comparatively modest thermal circuit for a spa-first ranking
- Macdonald Cardrona — credible leisure/golf spa, but less destination-spa depth than the selected Scottish options

Dalhousie Castle was excluded because its spa is under refurbishment with reopening indicated for 2027.

## Interpretation notes

- Prices are indicative research estimates, not live quotes, and can move substantially by date, room category and package.
- Booking lead times are practical recommendations rather than contractual minimums unless a hotel explicitly states otherwise.
- Drive times are approximate planning ranges from central Newcastle upon Tyne. Central Edinburgh traffic and Lake District approach roads can materially increase journey time.
- Coordinates for newly added records are address-level map estimates and are suitable for shortlist navigation; Directions buttons use the hotel name and full address in Google Maps.
- Rockliffe Hall had non-resident spa/treatment restrictions through 6 Sep 2026, with spa breaks due to resume 7 Sep; check current operations before booking.
- Matfen Hall's Aurelius Spa is scheduled to open 15 Sep 2026. Its existing hotel review score must not be treated as independent validation of the new spa.
- Prices and operational caveats are the most time-sensitive fields and should be refreshed most frequently.

## Analytical score weighting

- 35% spa breadth and quality
- 20% independent guest rating
- 15% hotel and dining proposition
- 10% setting / wellness experience
- 10% driving convenience
- 5% value / price transparency
- 5% operational confidence
