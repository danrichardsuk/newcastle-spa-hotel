## Interpretation notes

- Prices are indicative and can move substantially by date, room category and package.
- Booking lead times are practical recommendations rather than guarantees unless explicitly stated by a hotel.
- Drive times are approximate from Newcastle upon Tyne in normal traffic.
- Coordinates are intended for shortlist navigation; the Directions buttons use the hotel name and full address in Google Maps.
- Matfen Hall's Aurelius Spa is scheduled to open 15 September 2026, so its existing hotel review score should not be treated as an independent review of the new spa itself.
- Rockliffe Hall had spa refurbishment restrictions affecting treatments/non-resident access through 7 September 2026; users should confirm current operations before booking.
