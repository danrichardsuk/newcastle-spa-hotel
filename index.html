How the value index works It uses the estimated total spa-break cost for two against the research quality score, with a modest independent-review adjustment where a comparable rating is available. The index is only awarded to the recommended and value/high-secondary tiers, so cheap leisure-only hotels do not dominate the result. Price estimates are midpoints or representative published/package prices, not live quotes.

The exhaustive audit found 78 plausible candidates. Research scores use the latest audit framework: 30% spa depth/facilities, 25% independent guest evidence, 15% hotel quality, 10% spa integration/serenity, 10% value, 5% Newcastle accessibility and 5% evidence/operational confidence. Scores of 84+ form the recommended list. Cost filtering uses a normalised estimated total for two extracted from the researched price guidance; hotels without a comparable estimate are excluded when a budget ceiling is selected. The separate value index compares quality with estimated cost and should be treated as a purchasing aid rather than a replacement for the main spa-quality ranking.
